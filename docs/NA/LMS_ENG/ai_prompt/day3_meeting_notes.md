# Online expert consultation service planning meeting minutes (July 1st week)

* **Purpose of the meeting**: Define key service user behavior scenarios
* **Service Flow**:
  1. General members sign up using their email address and nickname. The default status when signing up is 'active'.
  2. Professional partners complete counselor registration by registering their name, counseling specialty (category), and self-introduction.
  3. Users view the list of registered experts and apply for a consultation appointment on the desired date. When applying for a reservation, the reservation status is set to 'waiting'.
  4. Once a reservation request is received, payment for the reservation details is required. The payment method (credit card, etc.) and payment amount are recorded in the payment table, and once the payment is approved, the reservation status is updated to 'confirmed'.

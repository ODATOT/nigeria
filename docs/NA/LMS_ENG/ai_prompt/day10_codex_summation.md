# Day 10 Training Content Summary (Codex AI Workspace)

This summary is the final comprehensive summary guideline for instructors and participants conducting the Day 10 **"Service Launch"** training session. Day 10 is the last day of the development process, where you don't write a single line of code. Instead, we work with Codex AI to make all the necessary preparations to release the completed service to the world. This is a time to experience the work that actual service planners and PMs do right before launching.

---

## 📌 Part 1. Introduction background and necessity

### 1. Why Day 10 should be the service launch

Here's a look back at the 10-day training flow so far:

| date | Topic |
|------|------|
| Day 1~2 | Planning and materializing ideas |
| Day 3~4 | Requirements and Design |
| Day 5~6 | UI/UX and screen development |
| Day 7 | Feature completion (backend connectivity) |
| Day 8 | Member Services |
| Day 9 | Payment service |
| **Day 10** | **Service Launch** ✅ |

The last part of this flow naturally becomes **"the process of releasing the service to the world"**. No matter how great your service is, if you don't introduce it well to users, no one will use it.

### 2. What planners actually do before launching

A real service planner’s launch checklist:
* Writing and reviewing service introductions
* Write opening notice text (email, homepage, SNS)
* Write FAQ
* Preparation of customer service guide
* Write a result report
* Establishment of next roadmap

We do all of this together with Codex.

## 📌 Part 4. Follow along (Step-by-Step practice guide)

### STEP 1. Task 01 - Preparing for service launch

* **Action**: Create `day10_service_overview.md` that summarizes basic service information and request Codex to write an introduction.
  > "Please write a service introduction so that users who use our service for the first time can understand the core value within 5 seconds. Include the service name, three core functions, target users, and differences."
* **Check**: Review whether it's written around benefits, without technical jargon, and whether readers will want to use it right away.

### STEP 2. Task 02 - Checking the service from the user’s perspective

* **Action**: Deliver the list of service features to Codex and request the creation of an FAQ.
  > "Create the 20 most common questions asked by users who are using this service for the first time in FAQ format. Divide them into five categories: membership registration, service use, payment, error resolution, and other."
* **Check**: Check that it contains questions that real novice users would ask and that the answers are specific enough.

### STEP 3. Task 03 - Organizing project performance

* **Action**: Create `day10_project_summary.md` that summarizes the 10-day development process and request a report on the results.
  > "Please organize the most disappointing aspects of this project and the features that need to be improved in the next version by MoSCoW priority."
* **Check**: Review whether the retrospective is honest and specific and whether the Version 2.0 roadmap is feasible.

### STEP 4. Mission 01 - Create a service launch package

* **Action**: Consolidate all materials created in Task 01-03 and request final inspection from Codex.
  > "Make people who read your service introduction want to use the service right away. If there are any parts that are too rigid or technical, edit them to make it more friendly and persuasive."
* **Ensure**: Ensure that all documents in the launch package have a consistent tone and message.

### STEP 5. Mission 02 - Final presentation of the project

* **Action**: Create a table of contents for your presentation and request feedback from Codex.
  > “Where is the least persuasive part of this presentation? What questions do investors most want to ask?”
* **Check**: Final check to see if the core value and performance of the service can be clearly communicated within 10 minutes.

## 📌 Part 5. Instructor guidance (topic of discussion)

**Discussion 1. The future of communication with AI**
> "What was the most surprising moment during the 10 days of collaborating with Codex? In what work do you think you will utilize AI the most in the future?"

**Discussion 2. Differences in perspectives of service planners and developers**
> “As you were preparing to launch the service, what did you feel was the biggest difference from a developer’s perspective and a planner’s perspective?”

**Discussion 3. If we create Version 2.0**
> “If you were to continue this project for three more months, what is one feature you would absolutely like to add? Why?”

**Discussion 4. Limitations of AI collaboration and ways to overcome it**
> "Have you ever experienced Codex leading you in the wrong direction? How did you fix it?"

## 📌 Part 6. Day 10 Key Message

> **"AI is not a tool, but a partner in planning together."**

During the 10-day training, students experienced:

* Day 1-5: How to **Structure Your Thoughts** with AI
* Day 6~9: How to **create a service** with AI
* Day 10: How to **bring your services to the world and prepare** for what’s next with AI

Creating the final Version 2.0 roadmap is not the end of training, but the **beginning of the next project**. The most important goal of Day 10 is to experience AI not as a simple document creation tool, but as a partner in planning your next project.

Mission 01. Create a service launch package

The results of Task 01~03 are integrated to complete a complete launching package that can be distributed immediately on the opening day.

Mission 01 Service launch package creation practice guide

📥 Practice Input

  • • day10_all_tasks.md

💾 Practice output (Output)

  • • day10_launch_package.md

1. Story and practice background

The introduction, FAQ, and result report written in Tasks 01 to 03 have been prepared. Now it's time to put all this material together into one complete launch package. Assuming that you are actually releasing the service to the world, complete the launch materials to a level that can be sent to users today. We increase persuasiveness by asking Codex to ‘make people who read this introduction want to use the service right away.’

2. Learning objectives

  • The results of Task 01~03 can be integrated into one complete launching package.
  • The quality of service introductions, FAQs, notices, and manuals can be adjusted so that they have a consistent tone and message.
  • AI can be used to increase the persuasiveness of the draft and supplement missing content from the user's perspective.

3. Codex AI prompt card

Copy and paste the prompt below into Codex Client and receive the results.

Make people who read your service introduction want to use the service right away. If there are parts that are too rigid or technical, please edit them to make them more friendly and persuasive.
Review the entire introduction, FAQ, open notice, and user manual and unify any inconsistent terminology or tone. Also, if there are any important items missing, please add them.
If there are three reasons why potential customers who see this launch package would not sign up for the service, please tell me three reasons. And please suggest content or phrases that can resolve each reason.

Day 10. Service Launching Training Summary

The last day of the 10-day training journey - the entire process of releasing the completed service to the world, supporting users, and looking back on the project is summarized.

Looking back on the 10 days of training so far, the most natural ending is Opening the service to the worldIt is. No matter how great your service is, if you don't introduce it well to users, no one will use it.

📌 Part 4. Service launch journey flowchart

[Service completion] ➔ (one line definition of core value) │ ▼ (Task 01: Introduction + Notice for each channel + Terms of Use) [Prepare launch materials] ➔ (So that users can understand within 5 seconds) │ ▼ (Task 02: 20 FAQs + response scenario) [Preparing for user support] ➔ (Preventing a flood of inquiries on the first day) │ ▼ (Task 03: Result report + Retrospective + Roadmap) [Project Summary] ➔ (Delivering results to investors and stakeholders) │ ▼ (Mission 01: Full integration) [Launching package complete] ➔ (A level that can be distributed today) │ ▼ (Mission 02: 10 minute presentation + Q&A) [Final Announcement] ➔ “The next project will start with Version 2.0”

📌 Part 5. Instructor guidance (topic of discussion)

  1. The future of communication with AI: “What was the most surprising moment during the 10 days of collaborating with Codex? In what work do you think you will utilize AI the most in the future?”
  2. Differences between planner and developer perspectives: “As you were preparing to launch the service, what did you feel was the biggest difference from a developer’s perspective and a planner’s perspective?”
  3. If you create Version 2.0: “If this project continues for three more months, what is one feature you definitely want to add?”
  4. Limitations and overcoming AI collaboration: “Have you ever experienced Codex leading you in the wrong direction? How did you fix it?”

📌 Part 6. Day 10 Key Message

💡 “AI is not a tool, but a partner in planning together.”

Day 10 is not just the end of a project; Service launch → Preparation for user support → Sharing of results → Retrospective and next planningIt is completed with training to perform the actual work of a service planner with AI (Codex). Creating the final Version 2.0 roadmap is not the end of training, but the beginning of the next project.

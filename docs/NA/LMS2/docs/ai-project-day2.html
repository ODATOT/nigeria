Opening question

“AI야, Jumia 같은 앱 만들어 줘.”
그러면 Jumia가 나올까요?

AI는 몇 분 만에 상품 목록, 장바구니, 리뷰 화면을 만들어 줍니다. 그런데 그 앱은 성장할까요?

AI가 잘 만드는 것

  • 보이는 화면 (UI 레이아웃, 버튼, 카드)
  • 일반적인 기능 (검색, 장바구니, 로그인)
  • “평균적인” 쇼핑몰의 구조

AI가 스스로 만들지 못하는 것

  • 왜 그 순서로 정보를 보여 주는가 (의도)
  • 무엇이 사용자를 다시 오게 하는가 (루프)
  • 수년간의 실험으로 다듬어진 결정 (데이터)
화면은 복사할 수 있어도, 성장의 이유는 복사되지 않습니다. 그 이유를 찾는 기술이 그로스해킹입니다.

What is growth hacking?

그로스해킹 = 성장을 위한
데이터 기반 빠른 실험

2010년 Sean Ellis가 처음 쓴 용어. 마케팅·제품·데이터·개발을 넘나들며 “무엇이 성장을 만드는가”를 가설과 실험으로 찾아내는 방법입니다.

“해킹”은 불법 침입이 아니라, 적은 자원으로 성장을 만드는 창의적인 지름길이라는 뜻입니다.
목표오직 하나, 지속 가능한 성장
근거느낌이 아니라 데이터와 사용자 행동
방법작고 빠른 실험을 반복
수단광고뿐 아니라 제품 자체를 바꿈
Traditional marketing vs Growth hacking

성장 장치는 제품 안에 숨어 있다

전통적 마케팅

  • 목표: 인지도, 노출, 유입
  • 범위: 퍼널의 맨 앞 (Acquisition)
  • 방식: 큰 예산 캠페인, 결과는 사후 확인
  • 담당: 마케팅팀

그로스해킹

  • 목표: 유입 → 활성화 → 유지 → 추천 → 수익 전체
  • 범위: 사용자 여정 전체 (AARRR)
  • 방식: 작은 실험을 빠르게, 지표로 판단
  • 담당: 기획·개발·디자인·데이터가 함께
  • 후기 수·평점 표시 → 구매 불안 감소 장치
  • “다음 동영상” 자동재생 → 체류·재시청 장치
  • 판매자 인증 배지·피드백 → 거래 상대 신뢰 장치
  • 친구 초대 보상 → 추천·유입 장치

From funnel to growth loop

퍼널은 끝나지만, 루프는 스스로 돈다

성공한 서비스는 사용자의 행동이 서비스의 가치를 키우고, 그 가치가 다시 새 사용자를 데려오는 순환 구조를 가집니다.

반드시 함께 쓸 것: 루프가 멈추는 조건 (예: 내 지역에 매물이 없으면 아무도 다시 오지 않는다)
YouTube시청 → 취향 데이터 축적 → 더 정확한 추천 → 더 오래 시청
Jiji판매자가 광고 등록 → 지역 매물 증가 → 더 많은 구매자 방문 → 더 많은 판매자 등록
Jumia구매 후 평점·후기 작성 → 신뢰 정보 증가 → 새 구매자 불안 감소 → 구매·후기 증가

The growth experiment cycle

성장은 아이디어가 아니라 실험의 반복

01 · 분석지표와 사용자 행동에서 이탈·기회 지점 찾기
02 · 아이디어“~하면 ~가 오를 것이다” 가설을 많이
03 · 우선순위ICE 점수: Impact · Confidence · Ease
04 · 실험A/B 테스트 등 작게 빠르게, 결과로 학습

좋은 실험 가설

EXP-001
가설: 상품 카드에 후기 수를 보여 주면
      상세페이지 진입률이 오를 것이다
지표: 카드 클릭률 (부작용: 이탈률)
성공 기준: 클릭률 +10% 이상

오늘의 핵심 통찰

  • 우리가 보는 완성된 화면 = 수많은 실험 중 살아남은 결과
  • 실패한 실험, 버려진 버전은 화면에 보이지 않음
  • AI는 이 실험 기록과 데이터를 모름 → 평균적인 답을 냄

Why document?

With AI, the document is the product plan

AI writes code fast. But AI only knows what you give it. Document-driven development means: write down what, why and how first, then let AI build from the documents.

✕ Chat-driven: "just ask"

"Make a video app like YouTube."
"Add a subscribe button."
"No, make it look better."
"Why did you remove the list?"
  • AI guesses → you get the average app
  • Decisions are lost in a long chat
  • New chat = AI forgets everything
  • Teammates can't see why something was built

✓ Document-driven

docs/PRD_BM.md  what we build, for whom, done-when,
                what we fake + who pays (3 lines)
docs/OBS_LOG.md the evidence behind each feature
"Read the 2 attached files. Build F1–F3."
  • AI builds your design, not a guess
  • Same documents → same result in any tool, any chat
  • Teammates and investors can read the plan
  • When something breaks, you can trace why

1 · Memory

AI has no memory between chats. Documents are the memory.

2 · Contract

"Done when…" tells AI and the team when to stop.

3 · Teamwork

Three people, one plan. Nobody depends on one person's chat history.

4 · Investors

A POC with PRD + BM shows a business, not just screens.

"If it isn't written down, AI doesn't know it, your team doesn't know it, and next week you won't remember it."

Step 01 · Observe

Good OBS vs bad OBS

✕ "YouTube's design is clean and easy to use."

Opinion. Nobody can check "clean".

✓ "The Home screen shows 8 videos in 2 columns and one Shorts row."

Fact. Anyone can count it.

✕ "YouTube uses AI to track us and make us addicted."

Hidden motive. Not on the screen. Can't be proved.

✓ Fact: "After 3 cooking videos, the Home screen shows 5 cooking videos." Interpretation: "(hypothesis) Watch history shapes Home."

Fact and guess separated, guess labelled.

✕ "Subscribe, share and comments help retention."

Three facts in one card, no detail. Split into three cards.

✓ OBS-006 Subscribe + bell · OBS-009 Share panel · OBS-005 Comments

One card, one fact. Each can link to a different stage.

✕ "There's an ad and you can skip it." (no date, no device, no screenshot)

Can't be verified. Ads differ by country, device, time.

✓ "2026-11-25 10:20, Android app, logged out: a [15]-second ad, 'Skip' after [5] s. evidence/OBS-010-ad.png"

Checkable. Context + numbers + proof.

Step 01 · Observe · cautions

Five rules for honest evidence

1 · Use the three sessions as three lenses

SessionLook forYouTube
First visit
logged out		5-second promise, main button (CTA)Search bar, Home grid, "Sign in"
Typical taskClicks, scrolls, trust, where you want to leaveWatch → ad → next video
ReturnWhat brings you backSubscribe, bell, history, Home

Your yesterday notes probably cover one lens. Tag each card with its session and see which lens is missing.

2 · The disagreement test

Could a classmate disagree? → interpretation. Can anyone check it on screen? → fact.

3 · Hypothesis words

Algorithms, money, company policy: write "(hypothesis)", "it seems", "may", unless an official page says it. Add the link if it does.

4 · "Not observed" is a good answer

No evidence for Referral? Write Not observed. Never fill a gap with a guess, and never let AI fill it. AI invents facts.

5 · Privacy and copying

Blur names, faces, phone numbers in screenshots. Don't copy logos, images or text into your build. Public screens only: no hacking, no scraping.

Connecting yesterday

OBS → growth loop → EXP → a PRD feature

Yesterday's loop and ICE were not separate exercises. They are the bridge between "what I saw" and "what we build".

1 · The loop is made of OBS

[Viewer watches a video]            OBS-004
  → [YouTube learns what they like]  OBS-007 (hypothesis)
  → [Better "Up next" and Home]      OBS-004, OBS-007
  → [Viewer watches longer, shares]  OBS-009
  → [Friends arrive by link]         OBS-002  → back to start
Stop: recommendations feel repetitive; mobile data too costly

Every arrow carries an OBS ID. An arrow with no OBS = "(hypothesis)".

2 · EXP attacks the weakest arrow or a stop condition

Weak point: viewers on expensive data stop after one video.

EXP-001  If the autoplay countdown shows a Cancel button
         and the next video's length,
         then 2nd-video rate goes up 10%,
         because viewers keep control (OBS-004).
         Watch: complaints, early exits.   ICE 8·6·8 = 7.3

3 · The top EXP becomes a PRD feature, with its metric

PRD-F1  Up-next list + 5-second autoplay countdown with Cancel
        Based on: OBS-004, EXP-001   Stage: Activation
        Success metric: % of sessions with 2+ videos (from EXP-001)

So the CMP step later can ask: did F1 do what EXP-001 predicted? Sample: 24p_GROWTH_LOOP_EXP_sample.md

Why can't AI rebuild it the same way?

You see the menu, not the kitchen

Think of a busy buka (local restaurant) near an office area. Copy its menu board and plates, and you still don't have the restaurant.

Above the water: what you see

Menu board · prices · plates of jollof, amala, egusi · the counter · the queue at 1 pm

Below the water: what makes it work

Everything on the right. A new restaurant that copies only the menu usually closes within months, just like most new apps.

ExperimentsTried 5 soups, kept the 3 that sell. Changed the portion size twice. Ask: what was tested?
DataKnows regulars, what sells on Fridays, how much rice to cook. Ask: what has it learned over time?
OperationsMarket at 5 am, cooks, cleaning, the cashier who handles transfers. Ask: which people work behind it?
ContextOffice workers want food in 10 minutes, under ₦2,500. Ask: who uses it, and what worries them?
ConstraintsGas prices, rent, health rules, supplier deals. Ask: what money or rules limit it?
IntentFast, cheap lunch so workers come back every day. Ask: why is it like this?
"AI sees the menu board. If you don't tell it about the kitchen, it builds a menu board with no kitchen. What isn't in your documents doesn't exist for AI."

You can copy

The visible pattern: a countdown, a button, a list.

You can fake

Data and operations with mock data and simple rules. Say so in ADR.

You can't copy

Years of data, staff and contracts. Write it as an expected gap in CMP.

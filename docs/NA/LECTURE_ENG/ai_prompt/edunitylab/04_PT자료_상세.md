# [Day 8] Creating Member Services (User Management) (Slides 112–123)
- **Slide 112. Day 8 opening & 22nd session information**: [Creating Member Service] Session introduction and onboarding journey.
- **Slide 113. Introduction question**: Sign-up fatigue friction point that drove away all customers by placing 15 sign-up input fields while only thinking about database rules.
- **Slide 114. Key explanation**: A design method that removes onboarding barriers by splitting the process into immediate approval (step 1) and detailed information when applying for the first reservation (step 2).
- **Slide 115. Practice Task 01**: Brainstorm required sign-up/log-in (Must) and SNS alliance/withdrawal (Nice) and create a priority table.
- **Slide 116. Practice progress Task 02**: Inspect the friction block of the complex membership sign-up specification (`day8_signup_spec.md`) and establish a shortened improvement plan.
- **Slide 117. Practice Task 03**: `day8_user_service.js` Blocking duplicate email subscriptions, verifying consistency, and correcting single integration failure error phrase to defend against brute force hacking attacks.
- **Slide 118. Mission 01**: An improvement book that converges the embarrassment of a silver virtual persona in his 60s who was unable to use special character combinations in his password into a real-time validity indicator.
- **Slide 119. Mission 02**: Scoring operability, resilience, security multidimensional quality rating Usability Scorecard (`day8_usability_evaluation.md`).
- **Slide 120. Summary and Retrospective**: Key summary of Day 8 and payment management preview of Day 9.

# ODA Boost AI - ACE AI Startup Bootcamp Instructor Lesson Plan (Days 1–10)

## 1. Lecture operation principles

- **Lecture tone**: Concepts are delivered compactly with minimal theory, and practice (prompt guides instead of hand coding) and sharing are maximized.
- **Key message**: A planner is not someone who simply writes a rough plan, but a system engineer who designs an operable specification data pipeline and defends against project risks through collaboration with AI (Codex Client).
- **Process method**: Follow the ratio of 20% explanation, 60% practice, and 20% feedback and sharing.
- **Time distribution (total 10 days / 9 hours per day)**:
  - **Day 1**: Sessions 1 to 3 (Requirements definition specification pipeline)
  - **Day 2**: Sessions 4 to 6 (Consistency Verification, Change Management, and Decision Tracking)
  - **Day 3**: Sessions 7 to 9 (Technical Design - Data Normalization and ERD Modeling)
  - **Day 4**: Sessions 10~12 (Technical Design - API Design and Interface Definition)
  - **Day 5**: Sessions 13 to 15 (Service Design - UI/UX Review and Optimization)
  - **Day 6**: Sessions 16 to 18 (Development - Creating screens with AI)
  - **Day 7**: Sessions 19~21 (Development - Completing functions with AI)
  - **Day 8**: 22nd~24th (Member Services - Onboarding and Security Guard)
  - **Day 9**: 25th~27th (Payment service - Churn blocking and failure exception handling)
  - **Day 10**: 28th~30th session (service launch - packaging, FAQ and final roadmap announcement)

## 3. Detailed instructor guide & teaching tips for each session

### [Day 1] Requirements definition and specification pipeline
* **1st session: Preprocessing and object classification**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Familiarize yourself with Workspace concepts, 15-line executive summary (`meeting-notes.md`) and role family classification (Customer, Expert, Admin).
* **Second session: Design functional requirements and supplement normal/exceptional flows**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Creating a formal specification table based on requirements ID and supplementing exception flow (fail-safe).
* **3rd session: Separate questionnaire, check for linting, and send to Gmail**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Create open policy `open-questions.md` and send it to Gmail API after checking 6 AI Linter.

### [Day 2] Consistency Verification, Change Management, and Project Tracking
* **Level 4: Detection of multiple document inconsistencies (Consistency Check)**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Audit policy contradictions in meeting minutes, requirements, and screen designs with an AI planning review secretary (Reviewer).
* **5th session: Requirements change and impact analysis (Impact Analysis)**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Analyzing semantic diff before/after change according to planning revision and deriving the scope of influence of screen, API, and DB columns.
* **6th Session: Decision Tracking & Weekly Issue Report**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Decision log based on meeting transcripts, separation of outstanding issues, and automation of issue reports.

### [Day 3] Technical design (data normalization)
* **Lesson 7: Resolving gaps in data structure understanding and ERD modeling**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Generate Mermaid ERD code by deriving key entities and defining relationships from natural language scenarios.
* **Lesson 8: Standardizing terminology and sharing the impact of schema changes**
  - **Guide Tip**: Collect mixed vocabulary, build a standard glossary, and share the impact of schema changes.
* **Lesson 9: Design Decision Recording & Edge Case Exception Defense**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Normalization Design Resolution Logging and Concurrency/Soft Delete Reference Collapse Exception Safety Design Guidelines.

### [Day 4] Technical design (API design and interface definition)
* **Lesson 10: API contract writing and discrepancy auditing**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Building a RESTful-based standard API contract Req/Res JSON model based on screen UI configuration fields.
* **Lesson 11: API change impact tracking and global architecture review**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Review impact analysis of changes and RESTful rule violations (verb endpoints) and RFC 7807 error schema.
* **Lesson 12: API test scenario design**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Complete the Happy/Sad Path boundary value test case list verification checklist.

### [Day 5] Service Design (UI/UX Review and Optimization)
* **13th session: Beginner user UX review and UI consistency review**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Identify readability weaknesses and cognitive load requirements and suggest name/action color UI consistency guide.
* **Lesson 14: User Journey Optimization**
  - **Map Tips**: Screen-integrated design that shortens the 9-step movement route to 4 steps by applying accordion and simple consent.
* **Lesson 15: Persona research and comprehensive usability and convenience evaluation**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Collect persona logs of self-employed people in their 50s and create a numerical rating report for the four major usability quality indicators.

### [Day 6] Development (Creating screens with AI)
* **Class 16: Quickly creating a screen draft (Boilerplate & Layout)**
  - **Guidance Tips**: This is the step to break the darkness of the desktop editor. Just throw your layout specification to Codex and have it automatically create a responsive HTML/CSS skeleton.
* **Lesson 17: Debugging asynchronous data communication runtime errors**
  - **Teaching Tips**: Identify the causes of asynchronous delay errors such as `TypeError: Cannot read properties of undefined` and teach optional chaining (`?.`) or defensive conditional expressions.
* **Lesson 18: Code refactoring and review report writing**
  - **Tip**: Separate redundant helper functions from working spaghetti source, rename variables, and archive error reports (`day6_error_report.md`) and code review reports.

### [Day 7] Development (Completing functions with AI)
* **Lesson 19: Event binding and data loading**
  - **Map Tip**: Add an event listener (Submit, Click) to the static sign-up/reservation screen and connect the path to save information in the browser `localStorage`.
* **Lesson 20: Scenario information maintenance and input lock design**
  - **Map Tips**: Restore the predicament of losing all input data when going back with `sessionStorage` caching, and block reservation confirmation button repeated hitting errors with `disabled = true` lock.
* **21st session: Service completeness auditing and E2E final verification**
  - **Guiding Tip**: Embed the role of a virtual silver user in his 50s to derive the entire journey (E2E) E2E validation (`day7_e2e_validation.md`).

### [Day 8] Creating member services (User Management)
* **Lesson 22: Defining onboarding features and eliminating barriers to sign-up**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Prepare an implementation checklist for email sign-up/log-in and plan a segmented route that removes friction blocks from complex input forms.
* **23rd session: Reinforcement of duplicate registration guard and handling of login failure exception**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Review email duplication check logic and temporary link sessions for lost passwords, and unify them with an integrated error message that blocks hacker brute force when login fails.
* **Lesson 24: Onboarding FGI analysis of virtual users in their 60s**
  - **Guiding Tips**: Discover the need for a real-time effectiveness indicator of the sign-up form from the perspective of the digitally underprivileged and encourage them to complete a usability report.

### [Day 9] Designing a payment service (Payment Service)
* **25th session: Minimizing payment flow and planning order summary sheet**
  - **Guidance Tip**: Design a flow that integrates order, terms, and payment method into a modal overlay to reduce the payment flow until final completion to just 3 steps or less.
* **Lesson 26: Transforming PG raw error logs into action instructions**
  - **Guidance Tip**: Replace raw error logs like `ERR_CARD_LIMIT_EXCEEDED` with friendly, reassuring Korean instructions that users can respond to immediately.
* **27th session: Receipt type price segmentation and double approval prevention**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Create a Mitigation Playbook that places a detailed benefit discount display and a 256-bit security badge, and blocks double transmission with a UUID unique key (Idempotency Key) valid for 30 minutes.

### [Day 10] Service Launch
* **Lesson 28: Service open packaging and promotional banner creation**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Establish a 5-second introduction that condenses core values ​​and notice text for newsletters and homepage banners to match the tone of each channel.
* **29th session: Drafting easy terms and conditions and building 20 customer support FAQs**
  - **Guidance Tips**: We plan customer response scenario scripts and FAQ materials that cover the five major domains, including registration, use, payment, and errors.
* **30th session: Project retrospective report and MoSCoW roadmap performance presentation**
  - **Guidance Tips**: Through Keep/Problem/Try retrospectives, we organize the MoSCoW priorities for the 3-month development roadmap for Version 2.0 and mutually share and reflect on the final project outcomes.

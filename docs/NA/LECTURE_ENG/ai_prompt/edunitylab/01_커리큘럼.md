# ODA Boost AI - ACE AI Startup Bootcamp practical training curriculum (Days 1–10)

## 1. Course Overview

- **Course name**: [ACE Startup SW/AI Pilot Course] Antigravity-based AI workflow design and agent construction
- **Education type**: PBL (project-based learning) and practice-based education
- **Training target**: Prospective entrepreneurs and planners with a mixture of SW non-majors and majors
- **Required tools**: 1 PC per person, Internet connection, Codex Client usage environment
- **Scope of this document**: Full curriculum design from Day 1 to Day 10 (Days 1–10)

---

## 2. Detailed curriculum summary by day (Days 1–10)

### Day 1. Codex Client-based requirements definition
- **1st session**: Setting up project context, preprocessing key summary of meeting minutes (step 0), and classifying user roles (step 1).
- **Second session**: Build functional requirements definition specification table (step 2) and supplement exception flows such as payment/reservation (step 3).
- **3rd session**: Separation of indeterminate questionnaires (step 4), self-linting of 6 AI Linter verification rules (step 5), and sending to Gmail API.

### Day 2. Verify Consistency, Manage Change, and Track Decisions
- **4th session**: Planning triangle contradiction auditing and defect detection between the three major specifications (meeting minutes, requirements, and screen design).
- **5th session**: Semantic diff analysis before and after changes according to planning revision and derivation of screen, API, and DB chain impact range (Impact Map).
- **6th session**: Decision log based on meeting transcripts, separation of outstanding issues, and automation of priority issue reports.

### Day 3. Technical design (data normalization)
- **Lesson 7**: Derive key entities (User, Expert, etc.) and cardinality (1:N, 1:1) from natural language scenarios and build Mermaid ERD.
- **8th session**: Establishing a project standard terminology dictionary (Glossary) and sharing the impact of schema changes through analysis of fragmented and mixed vocabulary transfer.
- **Lesson 9**: Architectural Normalization Design Decision Log and Concurrency/Soft Delete Reference Collapse Exception Safe Design.

### Day 4. Technical design (API design and interface definition)
- **10th session**: Building a RESTful-based standard API contract Req/Res JSON model by reverse inferring screen UI information.
- **11th session**: Cross-auditing of field/endpoint omission discrepancies between plan and API and RFC 7807 standard error architecture review.
- **12th session**: API test scenario planning to verify normal flow (Happy Path), abnormal input, limit exceedance, and authentication expiration.

### Day 5. Service Design (UI/UX Review and Optimization)
- **13th session**: Review cognitive load requirements (star rating hiding, etc.) from the perspective of novice users and align UI consistency of action buttons/labels.
- **14th session**: User Journey optimization that shortens the 9-step route to 4 by applying reservation calendar integration and simple consent.
- **15th session**: Persona research of self-employed people in their 50s, collection of trouble logs and evaluation of the Usability Score of the four major quality indicators.

### Day 6. Development (Creating screens with AI)
- **Lesson 16**: Responsive dashboard and card component HTML markup and CSS grid skeleton code creation in formal planning.
- **17th session**: Asynchronous data rendering 404 TypeError exception debugging and improved readability Refactoring redundant code.
- **18th session**: Create a runtime JSON parsing error report (`day6_error_report.md`) and AI code review feedback report.

### Day 7. Development (Completing functions with AI)
- **19th session**: Integration to load data into local storage by applying events (Submit, Click) to the static login form and reservation card.
- **20th session**: Prevent data reset when going back, verify session caching, and design a disabled lock device to prevent repeated reservations.
- **21st session**: Final verification of availability quality diagnosis report (`day7_service_audit.md`) and virtual silver user movement E2E scenario.

### Day 8. Creating member services (User Management)
- **22nd session**: Create a checklist for defining and implementing general email sign-up/log-in (required) and SNS simple sign-up (optional) functions.
- **23rd session**: UX flow improvement plan to resolve the friction block of complex input forms such as marketing information.
- **24th session**: Reinforcement of conditional expressions for duplicate email sign-up guards, unification of security login failure notification text, and analysis of FGI for onboarding people in their 60s.

### Day 9. Designing a payment service (Payment Service)
- **25th session**: Payment flow optimization that reduces the payment flow of email subscribers from product selection to final completion to just 3 steps or less.
- **26th session**: Convert raw error logs (ERR_CARD_LIMIT), such as limit exceeded and communication delay, into Korean notices for user behavior guidelines.
- **27th round**: Reinforced secure receipt UI with VAT notation, 256-bit encryption, and 7-day refund link, and double payment defense lock design.

### Day 10. Service Launch
- **28th session**: Write a 5-second introduction summarizing core values, establish newsletter and website opening announcement banner text.
- **29th session**: Easy Terms of Use/Privacy Policy draft guide and 20 customer support FAQs in 5 major areas of membership/use/payment/error.
- **30th session**: Share the final results after establishing the 3 major project Keep/Problem/Try retrospective results reports and the MoSCoW roadmap.

# Movie marketing point extraction agent practice task

## Assignment goal

Design an agent that analyzes synopses and audience reviews to create marketing points and copy to promote movies.

## Synopsis Sample

```text
Minjae, a young man who works at an old theater in a small local town, prepares for the last film festival to save the theater that is about to close. 
Meanwhile, they meet retired director Soo-hyeon, who used to make movies at this theater, and the two face their own wounds while restoring forgotten films. 
As the film festival gets closer, the villagers bring out memories related to the theater one by one, and Minjae realizes that the theater was not just a building but a place where people spent time.
```

## Audience review sample

| number | Reviews |
|---:|---|
| 1 | It was a quiet but lasting movie. I liked the emotion that the theater space gave me. |
| 2 | The plot was a bit slow and the middle part was boring. |
| 3 | The actors' eyes and music were impressive. |
| 4 | There were no major incidents, but there were many scenes that touched my heart. |
| 5 | It may be a bit boring for viewers who are not familiar with art films. |

## Steps to perform

1. Extract genre, core conflict, and emotional tone from the synopsis.
2. Classify favorable and dissatisfied elements from audience reviews.
3. Define two or more target audiences.
4. Derive 3 marketing points.
5. Create poster copy and SNS short form text.
6. Remove and improve phrases that do not match the tone of the movie.

What You Should Be Able to Do After 10 Days

Core Toolchain

AI Development Tools Used in the Course

The goal isn't to memorize tool names — it's to use each tool safely at the right stage of development.

C

Codex

A development partner that understands the codebase, builds, investigates errors, tests and reviews.

A

Antigravity

A development environment for quickly building and iterating on screens and features from design documents.

AI

Claude

Reviews long documents and alternatives, and structures requirements, designs and presentation messages.

G

gstack

Connects planning, engineering, design and QA reviews into the development flow.

Completion Checklist

Course Completion Criteria

The size of your final output matters less than whether problem, design, build and testing are connected — and whether your team can explain its decisions and reasons.

The core user flow really runs and can be demoed.
You can explain how requirements, design and build results connect.
You have evidence of testing the normal flow and key failure conditions.
You recorded what AI suggested separately from what your team finally decided.
You submit the finished project, docs, slides and a next improvement plan.

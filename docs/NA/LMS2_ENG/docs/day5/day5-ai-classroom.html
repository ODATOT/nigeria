UI/UX Design & Interaction Design with an AI Learning Helper (with Codex AI)

Day 5 learning content: go beyond the Wireframe stage to design high-fidelity UI/UX at real-service level, and apply a design system and state-based interactions to shape a user-centered service experience. In this lab you use Codex Client Workspace for a UX Audit that objectively analyzes Friction Points in screen design, UI/term consistency checks between planned screens, shortened User Journey design, struggle logs from an older persona, and quality scoring.

For: students who finished wireframe design
Bring: laptop, Figma account
Format: 2h screen review from the user's view / 3h UI consistency checks and flow improvement design / 1h persona quality check and evaluation report

Key Summary Notes

Day 5 Summary

Today's key goals

On Day 5 you go beyond the Wireframe stage to design high-fidelity UI/UX at real-service level, and apply a design system and state-based interactions to shape a user-centered service experience.

  • High-fidelity UI design: apply typography, color guidelines and grids
  • Build a design system: standardize shared reusable components like buttons, inputs and modals
  • State Design: define Default, Hover, Focus and active/inactive states
  • Interaction and transitions: connect screens smoothly and use Smart Animate
  • Usability testing and feedback: check flows by running the prototype
Learning tip: ask the AI chatbot on the right questions like "What's the key difference between High-fidelity and Low-fidelity UI?" or "How do I use Variants and Auto Layout when building a design system in Figma?"

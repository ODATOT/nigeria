Task 02. Review Payment Failure Scenarios

Translate error codes like over-limit and delay into reassuring user messages, and build a lock against rapid double payments.

1. Story and lab background

Right after the order payment page was released, complaints flooded customer support. One customer's card was just over its limit, but the screen suddenly showed 'Error 1042: Transaction Declined by Carrier'. Afraid the money had already left their account with nothing to show for it, they refused to pay. Also, during a network delay, a user anxiously pressed the pay button repeatedly, sending the same order approval query to the database three times in a row — a serious data defect that caused a double charge.

2. Learning goals

  • List 4 typical errors that happen during a payment transaction, like card over-limit, insufficient funds and network delay.
  • Replace raw technical codes with friendly text that removes customer anxiety and tells them what to do next.
  • Design prevention rules against double-charge attempts, like disabling the button and an idempotent order key.

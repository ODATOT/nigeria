[Notice] We'll cover your AI credit costs (up to $40 per person)

Key information
We will cover your AI credit costs up to $40 per person. Submit your payment receipt, and after we check it, we'll pay you back in cash.

Hello, everyone.
To help with the cost of the AI tools you use in class (AI credits, paid plans, and so on), the course team will support part of the cost.

After you pay for AI credits, please submit your receipt to the course team. Once we check it, we'll pay you back up to $40 in cash.

Support amount
$40 / max per person
Paid as
Cash
What to submit
Payment receipt
How to apply
  1. Pay for AI creditsFirst, pay for the credits or paid plan of the AI tool you use in class.
  2. Get your receiptPrepare a receipt (or a screenshot of the payment) that shows the date, amount, and service name.
  3. Submit to the course teamGive your receipt to the course team. After we check it, we'll pay you up to $40 in cash.
Please note
  • Limit: up to $40 per person. If you paid less than $40, we'll pay back the amount you actually paid. Anything above $40 is on you.
  • Receipt required: we can't pay you back without a receipt. Make sure the amount and date are easy to read.
  • Your own payment: support covers only AI credit costs that you paid for yourself.
  • Questions: if you're not sure how to submit or which services are covered, ask the course team.

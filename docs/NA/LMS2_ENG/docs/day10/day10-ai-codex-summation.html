Day 10. Launching the Service Course Summary

The last day of the 10-day journey - a full wrap-up of releasing the finished service, supporting users and looking back on the project.

📌 Part 1. Background - why Day 10 is the service launch

Looking back on the 10-day flow, the most natural ending is releasing the service to the world. No matter how great a service is, nobody uses it if it isn't introduced well to users.

Day 1~2PlanningIdea → business model

Day 7Finishing featuresAPI integration, event handling

Day 9Payment servicePayment flow, reassuring errors, trust UI
Day 10Service launch ✅Introduction, FAQ, retro, presentation

📌 Part 3. Mapping of lab outputs

  • Task 01: day10_launch_materials.md — introduction + opening notices per channel + draft terms of use
  • Task 02: day10_faq_guide.md — 20 FAQs (5 categories) + 5 customer support scenarios
  • Task 03: day10_retrospective.md — results report + Keep/Problem/Try retro + MoSCoW roadmap
  • Mission 01: day10_launch_package.md — complete launch package combining all materials
  • Mission 02: day10_presentation.md + day10_v2_roadmap.md — final slides + 3-month sprint plan

📌 Part 4. Service launch journey diagram

[Service finished] ➔ (define the core value in one line) │ ▼ (Task 01: introduction + notices per channel + terms of use) [Prepare launch materials] ➔ (so users understand within 5 seconds) │ ▼ (Task 02: 20 FAQs + support scenarios) [Prepare user support] ➔ (head off the flood of first-day questions) │ ▼ (Task 03: results report + retro + roadmap) [Sum up the project] ➔ (share results with investors and stakeholders) │ ▼ (Mission 01: combine everything) [Launch package done] ➔ (ready to release today) │ ▼ (Mission 02: 10-minute talk + Q&A) [Final presentation] ➔ "The next project starts with Version 2.0"

📌 Part 5. Instructor guidance (discussion topics)

  1. The future of communicating with AI: "What was the most surprising moment working with Codex over 10 days? In what work do you think you'll use AI most from now on?"
  2. Planner vs developer views: "Preparing the launch, what felt most different between the developer's and planner's views?"
  3. If you built Version 2.0: "If this project continued for 3 more months, what one feature would you most want to add?"
  4. Limits of AI teamwork and how to overcome them: "Did Codex ever lead you in the wrong direction? How did you fix it?"

📌 Part 6. Day 10 key message

💡 "AI isn't just a tool; it's a partner you plan with."

Day 10 isn't just the end of a project. It completes the course by doing a real service planner's work with AI (Codex): service launch → user support prep → sharing results → retro and next planning. Writing the final Version 2.0 roadmap isn't the end of the course but the start of the next project.

Mission 02. Final Project Presentation

Check your 10-minute slides with Codex, prepare for Q&A and set a Version 2.0 roadmap, then give the final presentation.

1. Story and lab background

This is the final presentation session that ends the 10-day journey. Each team presents its project, checks its slides with Codex and sets a roadmap for the next version. The goal isn't just showing what you built, but a talk that makes investors or customers think "I want to use this service." Show Codex your slides and get feedback: "Which part is least convincing?"

2. Learning goals

  • Build slides that clearly deliver the service's core value and results in 10 minutes.
  • Learn to find and fix weak points in your slides through Codex's feedback.
  • Set a realistic, specific 3-month roadmap based on the retro and MoSCoW priorities.

3. Codex AI Prompt Card

Copy the prompts below, paste them into Codex Client and get the results.

Which part of these slides is least convincing? What will investors most want to ask?
Prepare the 5 toughest questions most likely to come up in Q&A after the talk, with answers.
If we build Version 2.0 in 3 months, write a sprint plan of which features to build each month. Assume a team of 2 developers and 1 planner.

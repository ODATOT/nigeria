❓ Day 5 Self-Evaluation Comprehension Quiz

Q. What is the most critical verification step after deploying to a public hosting URL?
① Seeing "Build Succeeded" in the hosting dashboard is sufficient.
② Since it worked on localhost, skipping external verification is fine.
③ Open the demo URL in an incognito window or on an external mobile device and execute the entire user flow from start to finish.

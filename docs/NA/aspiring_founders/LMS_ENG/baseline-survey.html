🏢 Startup Status & Baseline Diagnostics

ACE Startups Supplementary Baseline Survey

We are conducting a supplementary baseline survey to assess the current business status of startups participating in the ACE program.
This survey is designed to examine business operation regions, company registration status, revenue, customer and employment status, as well as fundraising experience. Please have the team lead/CEO or a team member knowledgeable about this information respond once per team.

Response Deadline: Until further notice
Estimated Time: Approx. 6–8 minutes
Baseline Date: August 31, 2026
Target: 1 response per team (Lead / key member)
💡
Participant Notice & Data Utilization Purpose:
Your responses will be strictly used for ACE project monitoring, performance management, and curriculum improvement. We kindly ask for your cooperation and transparent reporting.

📋 Survey Objectives & Core Assessment Areas

Scope of Survey

This survey objectively diagnoses the current maturity and commercial viability of participating ventures, tailoring follow-up mentoring and technical enablement across 4 essential business pillars:

📍
1. Operational Location & Regional Hub

Identifies headquarters, regional branches, and target deployment markets across Nigeria and Africa.

📑
2. Corporate & Business Registration Status

Examines formal company incorporation status (e.g. CAC), entity types, and regulatory clearances.

📈
3. Revenue, Customers & Headcount

Captures active paid customer base, monthly/cumulative gross revenue, and full-time & part-time jobs as of August 31, 2026.

💰
4. Fundraising & Financing Experience

Assesses equity financing history, angel/VC investment, donor/grant backing, and bootstrapping capital.

⚠️ Response Guidance: To ensure accurate monitoring data, please submit figures based on August 31, 2026 metrics. To avoid duplicate entries, submit only once per team.
